# Superstore Sales Performance Analysis
## About the Project
How is a business performing across sales, profits, customers and regions?
This project uses Power BI to explore Superstore sales data from 2014 to 2017. Through interactive dashboards, it examines business growth, product category performance, customer contributions and regional sales comparisons.

## What the Analysis Reveals
- **Business Growth:** Sales increased by 46.88% between 2014 and 2017.
- **Profit Growth:** Total profit increased by 48.42% over the same period.
- **Order Growth:** Order quantity increased by 49.12% between 2014 and 2017.
- **Customer Segments:** Consumer contributed the highest sales ($1.16M), followed by Corporate ($0.71M) and Home Office ($0.43M).
- **Product Categories:** Office Supplies led order quantity with 23K orders.
- **Regional Performance:** Compared sales across four regional managers, with Anna Andreadi recording the highest sales at $0.73M.
## Dashboard Features
- Year and shipping mode filters.
- Sales and profit comparison across customer segments.
- Category-wise order quantity analysis.
- Country and regional sales breakdown.
- Top 10 customers by sales.
- Regional manager sales comparison.
